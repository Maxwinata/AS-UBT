# Regulatory & Operational Reference (JUKLAK) — UBT Dormitory

> **Language / Pilihan Bahasa:** [🇮🇩 Bahasa Indonesia (Primary)](JUKLAK_REFERENCE.md) | 🇬🇧 **English Version**

This document provides a summary of the legal foundation and technical implementation guidelines (JUKLAK) governing UBT Dormitory operations. The student information system and admission portal must strictly comply with the following regulations.

---

## 1. JUKLAK-02 (Dormitory Information System & Electronic Signatures)
Regulates the digitization of dormitory admissions and electronic agreement procedures.
*   **Electronic Signature (TTE):** Utilizes Uncertified Electronic Signatures (TTE-TT) combined with One-Time Password (OTP) authentication and an immutable Audit Trail (SHA-256 hash digests) to ensure document non-repudiation and integrity under Article 11 of the Indonesian Law on Electronic Information and Transactions (UU ITE).
*   **Parent / Guardian Consent Channels:** 4 legally recognized verification channels:
    1. Offline physical consent letter (wet-ink signature with physical stamp duty).
    2. Electronic consent via the Portal with OTP verification sent directly to the guardian's mobile number.
    3. Remote verification via recorded Video Call.
    4. Re-use of verified government scholarship (KIP) documentation.
*   **Provisional Residents:** If parental consent verification is incomplete upon physical arrival, the student is granted "Provisional Resident" status for a maximum of 30 calendar days.

---

## 2. JUKLAK-03 (Collective Contract Ratification & Stamp Duty Efficiency)
Addresses legal validity and stamp duty cost reduction on electronic dormitory contracts under Indonesian Stamp Duty Law (UU Bea Meterai No. 10/2020).
*   **Collective Ratification (Form F-22):** Students **must** append a physical wet-ink signature onto the Collective Contract Ratification Sheet (maximum 40 students per sheet) during in-person check-in at the dormitory counter. Room keys cannot be released prior to signing this document.
*   **"1 Document, 1 Duty Stamp" Rule:** The statutory Rp 10,000 duty stamp is affixed **only once** to the Collective Ratification Endorsement Sheet (F-22) signed by the Dormitory Director. Individual digital contracts (F-19) and Code of Conduct pledges (F-02) do **not** require individual duty stamps.
*   **Subsequent Stamping (*Pemeteraian Kemudian*):** If a legal dispute proceeds to court, the individual digital contract of the contested party is printed and stamped subsequently at the post office, with statutory administrative penalty fees covered by the Foundation.

---

## 3. F-19 (Dormitory Housing Contract)
*   **Minimum Length of Stay:** 6 (six) months. Early termination without documented academic leave or medical emergency incurs a penalty forfeiture of the security deposit (equivalent to 1 month of room charges).
*   **Security Deposit Schemes:**
    *   *Non-KIP (Regular):* 2x monthly rent (paid upfront, or in 2 installments if authorized).
    *   *KIP Scholarship:* 1x to 1.5x monthly rent (payable in up to 3 installments).
    *   *Prospective KIP:* 2x monthly rent (payable in up to 2 installments).

---

## 4. F-02 (Code of Conduct & Integrity Pledge)
*   **Zero-Tolerance Violations (Immediate Expulsion):** Possession or use of illegal narcotics, firearms/bladed weapons, alcoholic beverages, physical violence, harassment, bullying, and gambling.
*   **General Rules:** Strict prohibition of smoking (including e-cigarettes/vaping) across all dormitory premises, strict evening curfews, prohibition of opposite-sex guests inside rooms, prohibition of unauthorized overnight outside visitors, and strict prohibition of commercial business activities or electrical installation modifications in rooms.
